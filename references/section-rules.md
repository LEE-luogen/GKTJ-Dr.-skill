# Section Rules

## Fixed Structure
1. `引言`
2. `1.1 报告背景`
3. `2、数据信息分析`
4. `3、反馈意见分析`
5. `3.1 积极反馈`
6. `3.2 待改进反馈`
7. `4、优化建议`
8. `5、附件-问卷题目内容`

Do not add, remove, merge, or reorder sections.

## Chapter 1 and 1.1 Rules
- `引言` and `报告背景` must stay distinct.
- `引言` should:
  - establish disease / management context
  - connect the product to doctor-facing evaluation
  - introduce 3-4 representative dimensions plus total dimension count
  - close with report purpose
- `报告背景` should:
  - expand clinical management context
  - emphasize what doctors care about in practice
  - explain why questionnaire analysis is useful
  - avoid repeating the same sentence order used in `引言`
- Use the opening and closing modules in `expression-modules.md` so that introduction sections do not become identical across reports.

## Chapter 2 Rules
- One subsection per question.
- Subheading format: `· 维度名称`
- Title must come from the question meaning, not a direct copy of the question.
- Before writing the paragraph, classify the question into one of these patterns:
  - consensus-dominant
  - cautious-recognition
  - split-judgement
  - risk-attention
- Then choose matching phrasing from `expression-modules.md`.
- Each subsection must:
  1. reflect key percentages accurately
  2. explain doctor judgement or prescribing tendency
  3. connect to medical/pharmaceutical logic
  4. end with a restrained clinical management reminder
- Preferred length: 220-320 Chinese characters.
- Do not write low-information text like:
  - `A+B很高，C+D很低`
  - `整体较好`
  without explaining what doctors are actually recognizing or worrying about.
- Do not use the same opening pattern for every question.
- Do not turn every paragraph into `报比例 -> 下结论 -> 提醒` with identical wording.

## Chapter 3 Rules
- `积极反馈` and `待改进反馈` are thematic summaries.
- Do not repeat chapter 2 question by question.
- Each item should use a title plus one paragraph body.
- Use percentage evidence from chapter 2.
- Use title and body modules from `expression-modules.md` to reduce repeated wording while keeping the same structure.

## Chapter 4 Rules
- 4-5 suggestions.
- Every suggestion must map to a chapter 3.2 issue.
- Use restrained verbs:
  - `建议`
  - `可考虑`
  - `可进一步完善`
  - `可建立`
  - `可通过`
- Prefer varying action verbs with the suggestion modules in `expression-modules.md`.

## Chapter 5 Rules
- Preserve original question order.
- Preserve original option meaning.
- Only organize, do not analyze.
